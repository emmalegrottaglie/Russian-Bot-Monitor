# Notice for config/lexicon.csv

Most rows of `config/lexicon.csv` are derived from the dog-whistle glossary of Mendelsohn, Le Bras, Choi and Sap, "From Dogwhistles to Bullhorns: Unveiling Coded Rhetoric with Language Models", ACL 2023 (https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.845), file `data/glossary.tsv` in https://github.com/juliamendelsohn/dogwhistles, downloaded on 20 October 2026 (337 entries; SHA-256 4b997f1fe1ad59cc04b01c44f48c2e01c64f6cfa5ab403173f60b3f3c6539759). Only each entry's term, surface forms, persona, type, register and cited source are used; the glossary's usage examples are not copied. Review decisions and added terms are this project's own (see docs/CODED-COMMUNITIES.md and research/lexicon.py).
